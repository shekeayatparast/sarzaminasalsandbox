  1152→- User reported: entered username "admin" + password "admin12345", received the success toast "ورود با موفقیت انجام شد" but was NOT redirected to /admin — stayed stuck on /admin/login.

  1154→- Inspected /admin/login/page.tsx and found the redirect pattern:
  1155→    router.push("/admin");
  1156→    router.refresh();
  1157→  This same pattern was also present in /agent/login, /agent/register, and /agent/(panel)/orders/new.
  1158→- Root cause: race condition between router.push() and router.refresh() in Next.js App Router.
  1159→  • router.push() queues a client-side navigation (RSC fetch) to the new URL.
  1160→  • router.refresh() immediately fires a refresh of the CURRENT route (still /admin/login).
  1161→  • The refresh can win the race, leaving the user stuck on the login page even though the toast appeared.
  1162→  • Server-side log confirmed this: after POST /api/auth/admin/login 200, we saw GET /admin 200 (the push) AND GET /admin/login 200 (the refresh of the old URL).
  1163→- Fix: replaced the pattern with window.location.assign() which does a full-page navigation. This:
  1164→  • Bypasses the client-side router entirely (no race condition).
  1165→  • Guarantees the freshly-set session cookie (from the fetch Set-Cookie header) is sent with the next request.
  1166→  • Reliably lands the user on the dashboard.
